Action gate (Provision Trading Governor)

HARD holds cannot be overridden by a human approver. Spec-basis, credit-clearance, contract-on-file, the THREE regulatory-class certificate/licence/registration checks (food-safety, alcohol-excise, tobacco-excise/age-verification) and sanctions screening are independently recomputed, never trusted from the advisor's proposal; a real dispatch or invoice settlement is always a human trading supervisor's call, at every rollout phase.

OpGate
:order/intakephase-3 auto-commit when clean, no capital risk yet -- the ONLY auto-eligible op in this domain
:regulatory/verifyALWAYS human approval when clean · spec-basis independently checked against provisiontrade.facts, never fabricated
:delivery/dispatchALWAYS human approval · real warehouse dispatch act · credit-clearance, contract-on-file, regulatory-class-specific certificate/licence/registration and sanctions-screening independently re-verified, never auto at any phase
:invoice/settleALWAYS human approval · real invoice-settlement act (money moves) · sanctions-screening + double-invoice guard independently enforced, never auto at any phase
